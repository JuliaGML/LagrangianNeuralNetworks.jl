Comparison

The four methods of the package, each trained with one fixed protocol on five benchmarks and measured with the package's measures of a learned model, which do not depend on its gauge. The tables below are the committed results of the script scripts/comparison/compare.jl: the page reads them when the manual is built, and trains nothing.

The protocol

Every benchmark is a Lagrangian $L(q, \dot{q})$ with $L(0) = 0$, $\partial_{\dot{q}}L(0) = 0$ and $\det\partial^2L/\partial\dot{q}^2(0) = 1$:

benchmark$L$$n$box of $[q; \dot{q}]$$\Delta{}t$rollout from
harmonic oscillator$(\dot{q}^2 - q^2)/2$1$[-1, 1]^2$0.1$[0.5, 0]$
coupled oscillator$\frac12\lVert\dot{q}\rVert^2 - \frac12\lVert{}q\rVert^2 + 0.1\,q_1q_2$2$[-1, 1]^4$0.1$[0.5, -0.3, 0, 0]$
pendulum$\dot{q}^2/2 - (1 - \cos q)$1$[-1.2, 1.2]^2$0.1$[0.8, 0]$
Hénon–Heiles$\frac12\lVert\dot{q}\rVert^2 - \frac12\lVert{}q\rVert^2 - 0.8(q_1^2q_2 - q_2^3/3)$2$[-0.4, 0.4]^4$0.1$[0.3, -0.2, 0, 0.1]$
double pendulum$\dot{q}_1^2 + \frac12\dot{q}_2^2 + \dot{q}_1\dot{q}_2\cos(q_1 - q_2) - 2(1 - \cos q_1) - (1 - \cos q_2)$2$[-1, 1]^4$0.05$[0.5, -0.5, 0, 0]$

Each network has one hidden layer of width 4 and the activation softplus, in Float64, and is trained with Adam at the step size linesearch = 1e-2, Batch(20), for 200 epochs, from seed 1234; each method sets the seed again before it builds its network, so a row does not depend on the rows before it. The acceleration data are the $21 \times 21$ grid of the box for $n = 1$ and 400 uniform samples of it for $n = 2$, with the closed-form acceleration of the benchmark. The position data are 10 trajectories of 50 steps for $n = 1$ and 20 of 20 steps for $n = 2$, from uniform states of the box, computed with the classical Runge–Kutta method at the step $\Delta{}t/20$ and kept every $\Delta{}t$.

The four methods:

  • LNNLoss: LagrangianNeuralNetwork(2n; width) on the acceleration data;
  • EulerLagrangeLoss: the same network wrapped in NormalisedLagrangian with base = zeros(2n), value = 0 and momentum = zeros(n), with the loss EulerLagrangeLoss plus ScaleCondition at zeros(2n) with the value 1, of weight 1, on the acceleration data;
  • VariationalMidpointLoss: LagrangianNeuralNetwork(2n; width) with its default regulariser MixedHessianRegulariser(arch, Δt; form = :tamed) of weight 1, on the triples $([q_k; q_{k+1}], q_{k+2})$ of the position data;
  • DiscreteLagrangianNetwork: DiscreteLagrangianNetwork(n; width) with DiscreteEulerLagrangeLoss(arch, Δt) and its default regulariser, on the same triples.

The columns of a table:

  • acceleration error: acceleration_error, the relative error of the acceleration on the $21 \times 21$ grid of the box ($n = 1$) or its $5^4$ grid ($n = 2$);
  • energy alignment: energy_alignment, the mean $|\sin\theta|$ of the angle between the gradients of the learned and of the true energy on the same grid, 0 where their level sets agree;
  • trajectory error: trajectory_error, the largest distance between a variational_rollout of 100 steps of $\Delta{}t$ from the first two positions of the reference trajectory and that trajectory;
  • training time (s): the seconds from the construction of the loss to the end of train!;
  • final loss: the last entry of the loss history of train!, the mean of the minibatch losses of the last epoch, whose scale differs from one loss to another;
  • status: ok; diverged where the rollout throws or is not finite, with no trajectory error; failed where the training or the measuring throws, or the final loss, the acceleration error or the energy alignment is not finite, with no acceleration error, energy alignment, trajectory error or final loss.

A continuous network is rolled out as the midpoint discrete Lagrangian DiscreteLagrangian(nn, Δt). The DiscreteLagrangianNetwork is rolled out as itself, and measured through inverse_modified_lagrangian(nn, Δt), whose gradient and Hessian the script takes by central differences of step $10^{-4}$. Where the velocity Hessian of that Lagrangian is nearly singular, its acceleration error depends strongly on the step. On the coupled oscillator, the velocity Hessian of the trained network, taken with the steps $10^{-2}$ and $10^{-3}$, has a condition number of about $7 \times 10^4$ at its worst point of the grid and about 70 at the median, and the acceleration error is $1.49 \times 10^5$ at the step $10^{-4}$, $1.09 \times 10^5$ at $10^{-3}$ and $4.2 \times 10^4$ at $10^{-5}$. A — marks a value the run does not have.
